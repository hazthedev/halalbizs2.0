# HalalBizs Seller Guide

This guide shows how to open a shop on HalalBizs, list products, register halal certificates, handle orders and returns, and get paid. Button and menu names are written in **bold** exactly as the Seller Centre shows them.

> Today HalalBizs is in listing-only mode: buyers can browse your products and certificates, but cannot add to cart or check out. Your **Also available in** links are how buyers reach you meanwhile. Online payment for buyers is still in development and runs on a simulator. Check with the HalalBizs team before telling buyers they can pay online.

## 1. Apply to become a seller

1. Create a buyer account and verify your email (see the Buyer Guide). You can also start from **Open a shop** on the seller sign-in page, which asks you to create the account first.
2. Select **Sell on HalalBizs** in the footer at the bottom of any storefront page. Signed out, it opens the seller sign-in page: sign in there, or select **Open a shop** to create the account first, and you then land on **Become a seller**. Signed in as a buyer, it opens **Become a seller** directly. Once you are an approved seller, it opens the Seller Centre. The page says applications are reviewed within 2 to 3 business days. The **Commission** box shows what HalalBizs takes from each sale, for example "10% of each sale", or a range "by category" when categories have their own rates. <!-- SHOT: Become a seller form -->
3. Under **Shop details** fill in **Shop name** (it must be unique, and the page shows your **Shop URL**), **Description** and **State**.
4. Tick **My business is SST-registered** if it is, and enter the **SST registration number**.
5. Under **Bank details** choose the **Bank**, enter the **Account holder name** and the **Account number** (8 to 17 digits, no spaces or dashes). Payouts go to this account.
6. Under **Documents** upload the **SSM certificate** and the **IC copy (owner)**. PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 4MB each.
7. Tick the confirmation box that your information is accurate and that you agree to the marketplace seller terms, then select **Submit application**.
8. The page **Application received** shows your shop is in the review queue. You get an email as soon as a decision is made.
9. If the page says **Application rejected**, read the **Reason**, fix the issue and select **Re-apply**. Re-applying deletes the old application and its documents, so you start fresh.
10. If the page says **Your shop is suspended**, buyers cannot see your products and you cannot use the Seller Centre. Contact support.
11. When approved, **Seller centre** appears in your account menu. You can also sign in at the seller sign-in page.

## 2. Sign in and the new-device check

1. Open the seller sign-in page, enter **Email** and **Password**, and select **Log in**.
2. If you have turned on two-factor sign-in under **My account > Profile > Security**, the **Two-factor check** page asks for a 6-digit code from your email or authenticator app. Enter it and select **Verify and log in**. Tick **Trust this device for 30 days** only on your own device.
3. When you sign in from a device HalalBizs has not seen, an email "New login to your HalalBizs account" tells you the device, IP and time. If it was not you, change your password and select **Log out other devices**.
4. When you sign in from a new device and two-factor sign-in is off, HalalBizs asks for a code before letting you in. The **Two-factor check** page says "You're signing in from a new device. Enter the code we emailed to confirm it's you." Open the email "Your login code", enter the 6-digit code (it expires in 10 minutes) and select **Verify and log in**. Select **Email me a new code** if it expired. This code email replaces the alert in step 3. The first device you ever use does not need a code.

## 3. Seller Centre menu

1. Open **Seller Centre**. The menu has: **Dashboard**, **Products**, **Halal certificates**, **Orders**, **Messages**, **Vouchers**, **Group buys**, **Live shopping**, **Earnings**, **Reviews**, **Questions**, **Shop settings**, and **Get help**. **View storefront** opens your public shop.
2. The **Dashboard** shows **Today's orders**, **To ship**, **Live products**, **Low stock**, **Available balance**, **On hold**, **Gross (period)**, **Commission (period)**, **Revenue over time**, **Orders by status**, **Top products** and **Recent orders**. Use the period buttons (for example 7 days) to change the range.
3. The dashboard also shows **My WHIP applications** for shops on the WHIP certification programme. Select **Refresh status** to load them, and **Follow application** to link its certificate and products automatically when WHIP certifies it. If your shop has no certificate yet, **Get certified** opens the WHIP sign-up page.

## 4. Shop settings

1. Open **Shop settings**. The **Commission** box at the top shows your rate. Each order page also shows the rate it was charged (**Platform commission rate**). Under **Profile** change **Logo**, **Banner**, **Description** and **State**, then select **Save profile**. To rename the shop, contact support.
2. **Holiday mode**: turn it on to pause orders. Products stay visible but cannot be bought. Turn it off when you return.
3. Under **Shipping** choose **Shipping fee mode**: **Flat fee for all of Malaysia** or **Per-state fees**. Enter **Shipping fee (RM)**, or fees per **State** (**Apply to all** copies one fee). **Free shipping over (RM, optional)** makes bigger orders ship free. Select **Save shipping**.
4. Under **Bank details** change the **Bank**, **Account holder name** and **Account number**, then select **Save bank details**. Each payout copies the details at the time you request it.

## 5. Add products and variants

1. Open **Products**. Search by name or SKU, filter by status or category, and spot **Low stock**. <!-- SHOT: Products list -->
2. Select **Add product** (or **Add your first product**). The page title is **Add product**.
3. Under **Basics** write **Product name (English)** and **Description (English)**. Malay and Vietnamese names and descriptions are optional and fall back to English.
4. Choose **Category** in three steps: **Top category**, **Sub-category**, **Final category**. Add **Brand (optional)**, **Condition** and **Tax class**. SST is only charged when your store is SST-registered. Tag **Attributes** so buyers can filter.
5. Under **Halal & product details** add optional trust details such as ingredients, expiry and your halal certificate (see section 6).
6. Under **Images** add up to the shown maximum of square pictures, 4MB each. Use **Move image earlier** and **Move image later** to set the first picture. Add one optional **Video** (MP4 or WebM, 30MB).
7. Under **Variations**, leave **This product has variations** off for one item, and enter **Price (RM)**, **Sale price (RM, optional)**, **Stock** and **SKU (optional)**.
8. For variations, tick **This product has variations**. Name **Option 1** (for example Colour) and add values with **Add**. Select **Add a second option** for Size. A table lists every combination. Set price, sale, stock, SKU and image for each, or use **Apply to all** (**Set**) for the same value.
9. **Sale schedule (optional)**: **Sale starts** and **Sale ends** apply to every variant that has a sale price.
10. Under **Shipping** enter **Weight (g)**, **Length (mm)**, **Width (mm)** and **Height (mm)**. Tick **Allow cash on delivery (COD)** if buyers may pay on delivery.
11. Under **Also available in** you can add links to your listings elsewhere (**Add a link**). Buyers get one button that opens your link. While the marketplace is listing-only these links always show.
12. Select **Save as draft** to keep working, or **Publish**. If product approval is on, the button reads **Submit for review** and the marketplace team approves it first. A rejection comes by email with the reason, and the product returns to drafts.
13. In the product list use **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Boost**, **Delist** and **Relist**. Select products and use **Delist selected**, or **Delete drafts** (drafts only).
14. **Bulk import**: select **Download template CSV**, fill it in, upload it and select **Import products**. Every product imports as a draft. Review each one, then publish.
15. Boosts: in the product list select **Boost** on a live product (Boosts is not in the menu). On the **Boosts** page check the **Product**, choose **Days** and select **Boost now**. The fee is taken from your available earnings and is not refunded if you cancel.

## 6. Halal certificates and the 2 December 2026 rule

1. Open **Halal certificates**. Select **Register a certificate** (or **Get certified**). <!-- SHOT: certificate form -->
2. Fill in **Certificate number** exactly as printed, **Issuing body**, **Certificate holder**, **Standard**, **Company registration number**, **Issued on** and **Expires on**. Optional: **Facility**, **Scope note**, **Produced in a dedicated halal facility**, **Export paperwork available**.
3. Upload the **Certificate document** (PDF or photo, up to 4 MB). Only our reviewers see it.
4. Under **Products this certificate covers** tick only the products named in the certificate scope.
5. Select **Send for review**. The marketplace team approves or rejects it. A rejection gives a reason. Select **Fix and resubmit**.
6. Until approval, the halal badge does not show. Nothing is badged until an admin approves.
7. To renew, select **Renew or edit** on the same certificate. The badge pauses until it is approved again, but your products stay on sale. Renew at least three months before **Expires on**. The list warns "days to expiry".
8. From 2 December 2026, products in food, grocery, drink, cosmetics and pharmaceutical categories (marked "Required to list" by admin) cannot be published without an approved, valid certificate covering that product. A certificate you have just submitted holds the listing for 14 days while we review it.
9. If a product cannot go live, the form shows **This product cannot go live yet** with the reason and a **Go to Halal certificates** link. Typical reasons: no certificate, the certificate expired, or it is still being checked.
10. Cosmetics and pharmaceutical categories follow the same rule as food from 2 December 2026: no approved, valid certificate, no listing. Products with a certificate show the halal badge.
11. Before 2 December 2026 the same check only reports and does not block. The categories that need a certificate are **Food & Snacks**, **Groceries & Pantry**, **Drinks**, **Home & Living > Groceries**, **Cosmetics & Care** and **Pharma & Supplements**.
12. Find my WHIP certificates: enter your **Company SSM number** and select **Find my certificates**, then **Link certificate**. Use **Import product drafts** to create draft products from a linked certificate.

## 7. Handle orders

1. Open **Orders**. Tabs: **New**, **To ship**, **Shipped**, **Delivered**, **Completed**, **Returns**, **Cancelled**. Search by order number or buyer, filter by **Payment method** and dates, and **Export CSV**. <!-- SHOT: Orders tabs -->
2. A new paid order, or a cash on delivery order, lands in **New**. Open it to see the items, **Note from buyer**, **Ship to** address, **Totals** and **Platform commission rate: :rate%**.
3. Select **Confirm & pack**. The order moves to **To ship**. To pack many, tick orders and select **Confirm & pack selected**.
4. Print the **Print packing slip**.
5. To cancel, choose a **Cancellation reason** and select **Cancel order**. Stock returns and the buyer is told. You cannot undo it.
6. When you hand the parcel to a courier, select **Arrange shipment**. Choose the **Courier** (J&T Express, Pos Laju, DHL eCommerce, Ninja Van, City-Link, GDEX, or **Other** with **Courier name**) and type the **Tracking number** from the consignment note. Select **Mark as shipped**.
7. The buyer sees a **Track on courier website** button that opens the courier's own tracking page. Double-check the tracking number.
8. When the parcel arrives, select **Mark delivered**. The buyer then confirms with **Order received**, or the order completes automatically on the date shown ("Completes automatically on :date").
9. Completed orders release money to you after the return window (section 9).

## 8. Returns

1. Orders with a return request appear in **Returns** and show **Return request** with the buyer's **Reason**, **Details** and photos.
2. Answer before the **Respond by** time (48 hours after the request). Unanswered requests go to admin automatically.
3. To agree, select **Accept return**. The buyer ships the item back. When it arrives select **Item received**. The marketplace team then processes the refund.
4. To disagree, select **Dispute**, explain in **Why are you disputing this return?** and select **Submit dispute**. Admin decides.
5. A refund before completion is also taken out of your sale and the coins the buyer earned on it.

## 9. Earnings, ledger, statements and payouts

1. Open **Earnings**. You see **Available**, **On hold**, **In escrow**, **Pending payout** and **Paid out (lifetime)**.
2. **In escrow** is money released when buyers receive their orders. **On hold** is money from completed orders, released to **Available** when the return window closes (7 days after delivery).
3. The **Ledger** lists every entry with **Date**, **Type**, **Description** and **Amount**: sales, commission, refunds, payouts, boosts. Select a column heading to sort, use the search box to find an entry, and change the page size with **Rows per page** at the top right of the table. The footer shows "Showing x to y of z" on the left and the page numbers on the right. **Payout history** works the same way.
4. To get paid, check the **Paid to:** bank line (change it in **Shop settings** with **Update in settings**), enter **Amount (RM)** under **Request a payout**, and select **Request**. The minimum is RM50, and you cannot ask for more than **Available**. Only one payout can be in progress.
5. Payout steps: **Requested**, **Approved**, **Paid** (with a bank **Reference**) or **Rejected** (the money returns to Available). See **Payout history**.
6. If the balance is negative (for example commission owed on cash on delivery orders, or a refund after payout), the page shows the amount owed. Future sales repay it, or you can pay by bank transfer. Payouts resume when the balance is positive.
7. On the 1st of each month at 09:00 a **monthly statement** email is sent to shops that owe a balance.
8. **Export CSV** saves the ledger. There is no statements page; the monthly email is the statement.

## 10. Chat, reviews, questions and promotions

1. **Messages**: pick a chat from the list, write in **Write a reply…** and select **Send message**. Reply quickly. Do not share passwords or codes.
2. **Reviews**: select **Reply** under a review, write your answer and select **Post reply**. You can edit it for 24 hours, then it locks.
3. **Questions**: **Unanswered** questions show first. Select **Answer**, write and **Post answer**. Use **Hide** for unsuitable questions.
4. **Vouchers**: select **Add voucher**. Set **Code**, **Type** (amount, percent or free shipping), **Minimum spend (RM)**, **Quota**, **Per-user limit**, **Starts at**, **Ends at**, and keep **Active** on. Select **Save voucher**. A shop voucher applies only to your items. The voucher list has a search box, sortable column headings and a page footer. Tick vouchers to **Turn on selected**, **Turn off selected** or **Delete selected** (used vouchers are kept).
5. **Group buys**: select **Create deal**, choose **Product** and **Variant**, set **Group price (RM)**, **People needed**, **Team window (hours)** and **Deal ends**, then **Save deal**. Select **End** on a row to stop it, or tick active deals and select **End selected**. The list shows **Product**, **Size**, **Team price**, **People needed**, **Teams** and **Status**, with a search box, sortable headings and a page footer. Each member can buy **1 unit** at the group price. If a member's group order is cancelled or fully refunded before it is completed, that member can buy 1 unit at the group price again while the deal runs; the team stays unlocked.
6. **Live shopping**: select **New session**, then **Manage rail** to add products, **Go live** and **End**.
7. Use the bell (**Notifications**) to see new orders, returns and questions.
