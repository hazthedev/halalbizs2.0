# HalalBizs Buyer Guide

This guide shows how to shop on HalalBizs: find halal products, check certificates, pay, track parcels, and get help. Button and menu names are written in **bold** exactly as the screen shows them.

> Online payment is still in development. At checkout you will see "Online payment", but today it runs on a built-in simulator and no real money is taken. Cash on delivery works normally.

> Today HalalBizs is in listing-only mode. You can browse products, check certificates and chat with sellers, but **Add to cart**, **Buy now** and **Checkout** are hidden. Where the seller added them, **Also available in** opens the seller's listing on another site. Sections 5 to 9 apply once ordering is switched on.

## 1. Create an account and sign in

1. Select **Sign up** in the top bar. <!-- SHOT: header with Log in and Sign up -->
2. Fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Phone (optional)**, **Password** (at least 8 characters) and **Confirm password**.
3. Tick **I agree to the terms & conditions and consent to my personal data being processed under the privacy policy**, then select **Create account**.
   Or select **Continue with Google**. If this is a new account, you then see **Create account** with the same tick box: tick it and select **Create account**. No account is made until you tick it. An existing account signs in straight away.
4. Open the email we send you and select the verification link. If you did not get it, select **Resend verification email** on the **Verify your email** page. You must verify your email before you can check out.
5. To sign in later, select **Log in**, enter **Email** and **Password**, and select **Log in**. Tick **Keep me logged in** only on your own device.
6. If you forgot your password, select **Forgot password?**, enter your email, select **Send reset link**, and follow the link in the email.
7. If you turned on two-factor sign-in (see section 13), the **Two-factor check** page asks for a 6-digit code. Enter it and select **Verify and log in**. Select **Email me a new code** if it expired (codes last 10 minutes), or **Use a recovery code instead**.

## 2. Language and currency

1. In the top bar, use the language switcher. English, Bahasa Melayu and Tiếng Việt are offered when enabled by HalalBizs.
2. Use **Display currency** to see prices in another currency. This is for display only. Your order is charged in MYR, and checkout says "You'll be charged in MYR."
3. You can also save your **Language** and **Currency** under **My account > Profile**.

## 3. Find products

1. Select a category from the menu, or type in the search box (**Search products, stores…**). Suggestions show **Recent searches**, **Trending**, **Products**, **Stores** and **Categories**. Select **See all results for ":term"** for the full list.
2. On the list page, open **Filters** and narrow by **Category**, **Certifying body**, **Assurance** (certificate valid 12 months+, dedicated halal facility, export paperwork included), **Price (RM)**, **Rating**, **Ships from** and **Payment** (Cash on delivery).
3. Use **Sort by**: **Relevance**, **Latest**, **Top sales**, **Price: low to high**, **Price: high to low**.
4. Remove one filter with its chip, or select **Clear all**. Select **Load more** to see more products.
5. Open **Flash Sale** from the home page for time-limited deals. Each deal shows **Ends in**, how many are left, or **Sold out**.
6. Select a store name to visit the shop page. Use **Follow** to keep the shop under **Followed shops** in your account menu.
7. Select the chat icon (**Ask the concierge**) to ask the shopping assistant for ideas in English or Bahasa Melayu.

## 4. Check the halal badge and certificate

1. A product card shows **Halal certified** when the seller's certificate has been approved and is valid today.
2. Open the product. In the halal panel you will see one of: **Halal certificate verified**, **Certificate lapsed** (expired), or **Certificate not yet in force**. It also shows the certificate number, issuing body and dates.
3. Select **Read the record** to open the public **Certificate register**. <!-- SHOT: product page halal panel -->
4. You can also open **Certificate register** directly, type the **Certificate number** (for example MY-JKM-2140-118) and select **Verify**.
5. A valid result shows the holder, issuing body, validity, facility and **Covered SKUs**. The certificate covers only the products named in its scope, not the whole shop.
6. If you see **No record**, do not trust that number. Select **Report a listing** and tell Help centre support.
7. From 2 December 2026, food, grocery, drink, cosmetics and pharmaceutical categories cannot be listed without an approved certificate.
8. On the product page, **Ingredients & scope** and **Traceability** (facility, batch, certificate expiry) give more detail when the seller filled them in.

## 5. Add to cart

1. On the product page choose the options (for example colour or size) and set **Quantity**.
2. Select **Add to cart** (the button shows **Added**), or **Buy now** to go straight on. If stock is gone, select **Notify me when available** and we email you when it returns.
3. Select the **Cart** icon to see the mini cart, or open the full cart with **View cart & checkout**.
4. In the cart, items are grouped by seller. Use **Select all**, **Select all items from this seller**, or **Select this item** to choose what to buy.
5. Change amounts with the minus and plus buttons (**Decrease quantity**, **Increase quantity**). Select **Remove** to delete a line.
6. A line marked **No longer available** or **Out of stock** cannot be bought. If stock is lower than you asked for, the cart shows **Only :count left — quantity adjusted**.
7. Select **Checkout**. If you are signed out, the button reads **Log in to check out**. Your cart is kept in the browser.

## 6. Check out

1. Under **Delivery address**, check the address. Select **Change** to pick another, or **Add new address** if you have none. We deliver within Malaysia only.
2. Check each seller's items, quantity, price and **Shipping**. Shipping shows **FREE** when the seller's free-shipping amount is reached.
3. Write an optional **Note to seller** for each shop.
4. Under **Vouchers**, select **Select voucher** and pick one, or type a code in **Voucher code** and select **Apply**. You can use one platform voucher, one shop voucher (it applies only to that shop's items) and one free-shipping voucher. Remove one with its **Remove ... voucher** button.
5. If **Use my coins** appears, tick it to spend coins (see section 9).
6. Under **Payment method** choose **Cash on delivery** or **Online payment — FPX, cards, e-wallets**. Cash on delivery appears only when it is enabled, every item allows it, and the order total is not above RM500. If no method fits, the page says "No payment method is available for this order."
7. Check **Order summary**: **Items subtotal**, **Shipping total**, **SST** (when charged), voucher lines, **Coins** and **Grand total**.
8. Select **Place order** once. <!-- SHOT: checkout order summary and payment methods -->
9. The **Order placed** page shows your **Order number**. For cash on delivery it says how much cash to pay when the parcel arrives.
10. For online payment, select **Complete your payment**. While in development this opens a **Payment simulator** page with **Simulate successful payment**, **Simulate failure** and **Cancel**. No real charge is made.
11. An online order waiting for payment is under **To Pay** and expires after 60 minutes. Items go back into stock when it expires.

## 7. Vouchers

1. Vouchers are picked or typed at checkout (section 6, step 4). A voucher is checked again when you place the order.
2. A voucher may need a minimum spend, a date window, remaining quota, or a per-user limit. If it fails, the page shows the reason.
3. If you cancel an unpaid order, or it expires unpaid, the voucher use is given back to you.
4. The daily **Spin the wheel** under **Coins** can win a voucher (see section 9).

## 8. Group buy

1. On a product with a group deal you will see **Group buy**. Select **Start a group** to open a team, or **Join** an open group.
2. After starting, copy the team link with **Copy** and share it. The page shows how many have joined (for example "2 of 3 joined") and when the group **expires**.
3. When the group is full the page says **Unlocked!** Select **Shop now at the group price**, add the item to your cart and check out.
4. If the group does not fill in time, start a fresh one from the product page.
5. The group price covers **1 unit per member**. If your cart line has more, checkout stops and asks you to change it to 1. To buy more, place a separate order at the normal price after your group order.
6. If your group-buy order is cancelled, or fully refunded before it is completed, your place at the group price comes back. You can buy 1 unit at the group price again while the deal is still running. The group itself stays unlocked; once the deal has ended the group price no longer applies.
7. Group buys are blocked while the marketplace is in listing-only mode.

## 9. Coins

1. Open **My account > Coins**. You see **Coin balance**, expiry notes and **Recent activity**. (The menu item is hidden when the programme is off.)
2. Select **Check in today** once a day. Your streak grows and later days pay more: 5 coins on days 1 and 2, 10 on days 3 and 4, 15 on days 5 and 6, and 30 on day 7. Then the cycle starts again.
3. Select **Spin the wheel** once a day for coins or a surprise voucher.
4. You earn 1 coin for every whole RM1 of items in a completed order. If an order is refunded, the coins earned from it are taken back.
5. At checkout tick **Use my coins**. 1 coin takes 1 sen off. You can use up to RM50 of coins per order, and the order total never drops to zero from coins alone.
6. Coins expire 180 days after you earn them. The page tells you when.

## 10. Orders and tracking

1. Open **My account > Orders**. Tabs: **To Pay**, **To Ship**, **To Receive**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**, **Return/Refund**. Use **Search your orders** to find one. <!-- SHOT: Orders page tabs -->
2. On **To Pay**, select **Pay now** to finish payment or **Cancel order** to cancel the whole unpaid order.
3. Each seller's part of an order is its own order. Select **View details** to open it. You see **Order status** history, items, **Delivery address**, **Order summary** and **Payment method**.
4. When the seller ships, the page shows **Tracking** with the courier and number. Select **Copy tracking number** if needed, or **Track on courier website** to open the courier's own tracking page.
5. HalalBizs does not show courier checkpoints itself. The courier link is the way to follow a parcel.
6. When the parcel arrives and the status is **Delivered**, check it and select **Order received**. This completes the order. If you do nothing, it completes by itself 7 days after delivery.
7. Select **Download invoice** on an order that has been confirmed.
8. After completion, **Buy again** puts the items back into your cart.

## 11. Cancel, return and refund

1. To cancel, open the order while its status is **Confirmed**. Select **Cancel order**, choose **Pick a reason**, then **Confirm cancellation**. Once the seller starts packing (**Processing**), you cannot cancel. Contact support instead.
2. To return, open an order that is **Delivered** or **Completed**, within 7 days of delivery, with no earlier request. Select **Request return**.
3. Choose a reason, add details in **Tell us more (optional)** and add photos if useful. Select **Submit return request**. Only one request per order.
4. Follow **Return status**. The seller has 48 hours to answer. If the seller accepts, ship the item back. If the seller disputes, or does not answer in time, our support team decides.
5. When the status is **Refunded** the page says "Your refund has been processed." Cash-on-delivery refunds are recorded by HalalBizs. Online refunds go back through the payment channel and the bank may take time to post them.
6. If a return is **Rejected**, the page says it was reviewed and not approved. Contact support if you disagree.

## 12. Reviews, questions and chat

1. After an order is **Completed**, select **Rate order**. Give stars, write **Your review**, add up to 5 photos, and select **Post review**. You can also rate the seller's service.
2. On a product page, the reviews tab shows ratings, **Verified purchase**, and **Seller response**. Filter with **All** or **With photos**, and select **Helpful** on useful reviews.
3. Under **Questions & Answers**, type your question in **Ask the seller a question** and select **Post question**. The answer shows on the product page.
4. To chat with a shop, select **Chat** or **Chat with seller** on the product page or order page. Your chats are in **My account > Messages**. Write in **Write a message…** and select **Send message**. A chat can start about a product, and **Remove product from message** drops that context.
5. Never send a password or a code in chat.

## 13. Your account and security

1. **My account** has: **Overview**, **Profile**, **Addresses**, **Orders**, **Coins**, **Creator**, **Subscriptions**, **Messages**, **Wishlist**, **Followed shops**, **Notifications**. Some are hidden when a programme is off.
2. **Addresses**: select **Add address**, fill in recipient, phone, address, postcode, city and state, then **Save address**. Use **Set as default**, **Edit** or **Delete**.
3. **Profile**: update details and select **Save changes**. Change your password with **Update password**.
4. Under **Security**, turn on **Two-factor authentication** with **Use email codes** or **Use an authenticator app**. Save your recovery codes when shown. **Regenerate recovery codes** replaces them. Turn it off with **Turn off 2FA**.
5. Under **Active sessions**, you see where you are signed in. Enter your password and select **Log out other devices** to sign out everywhere else.
6. New-device email: when you sign in from a device we have not seen before, we email "New login to your HalalBizs account" with the device, IP and time, and add a notice in **Notifications**. If it was you, do nothing. If not, change your password and use **Review account security**. The first device you use is recorded without an email. Buyers get this alert only, not a code.
7. Under **Privacy**, **Download my data** gives a file of your profile, addresses and orders. **Delete my account** asks you to type DELETE and your password. Order records are kept for legal reasons.
8. The bell shows **Notifications**. Select **Mark all read** when done.
9. Save a product with the heart (**Add to wishlist**). Open **Wishlist** later. Saving does not reserve stock.
10. If you are in the **Subscribe & save** programme, **Subscriptions** lets you **Pause**, **Resume** or **Cancel**.

## 14. Help

1. Open **Help centre** from the footer to read articles. The footer also has **FAQ**, **Refund policy**, **Terms & conditions** and **Privacy policy**.
2. For a problem, sign in, open **Support**, select **New ticket**, write a **Subject** and **Message** (at least 20 characters, include the order number), and select **Create ticket**.
3. Reply in the ticket with **Send reply**. Select **Close ticket** when solved.
4. To sell on HalalBizs, see the Seller Guide. Start with **Sell on HalalBizs** in the footer.
